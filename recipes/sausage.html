sausage

Sausage is one of the world’s oldest and most versatile forms of processed food. Originally created as a clever way for ancient butchers to preserve scrap meats and stretch resources, it has evolved into a global culinary art form.

Ingredients

  • Meat & Fat
  • Salt & Seasonings
  • Binders & Liquids (Optional)
  • Casing

Steps

  1. Gather Ingredients and Equipment
  2. Cut and Chill the Meat
  3. Grind the Meat
  4. Season and Emulsify (Knead)
  5. Stuff into Casings
  6. Twist into Links
  7. Rest, Cure, and Cook
