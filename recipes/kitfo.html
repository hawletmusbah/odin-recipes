Kitfo is a traditional Ethiopian dish of finely minced beef seasoned with spices and clarified butter, originating from the Gurage people. It is often compared to Western beef tartare.

Ingredients

  • The Beef
  • Niter Kibbeh
  • Mitmita
  • Cardamom (Korerima)

Steps

  1. Prep the Meat
  2. Melt the Butter
  3. Mix the Spices
  4. Combine and Warm (Leb Leb Style)
  5. Serve Immediately
